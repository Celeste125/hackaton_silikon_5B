Mixing while filling

Keeps the feed from piling up under the chute. Above about 60% the mixer shakes the scale, and the silos may stop early.

Feeding

Laps one at a time until the container is down to that weight. Door and mixer as set here; gaps and speed from the Feed tab. Docking needs Start (in front of the dock) and Docked saved in Setup.

Run

    Dose opens a valve until that much weight is added (needs live scale data, gives up after 2 minutes). Closing and cancelling work from any device.

    Shake to unjam

    Full speed one way, then instantly the other, with no ramp in between.

    100%

    Unequal times push the feed one way. Changes apply while it runs. A motor slider or STOP ALL ends it.
